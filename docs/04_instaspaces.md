# InstaSpaces by Venia Business Hub

Source: https://veniabusinesshub.com/instaspaces-by-venia-business-hub/

## Get Work Done. Wherever You Are

Access a network of workspaces in cafés and restaurants across Lagos. No ordering required at any location.

## What Is InstaSpaces?

InstaSpaces allows you to work from cafés and restaurants in multiple locations in Lagos with no long-term commitment required.

**Monthly fee: ₦45,000**

Members receive access to multiple venues for meetings, connections, and collaboration, plus periodic breakfast vouchers.

## Available Locations

- **Velocity Coworking Space** — Lekki Phase 1 (Mon–Fri: 9am–6pm)
- **Kaldi Africa** — Ikeja (Mon–Sat: 9am–5pm)
- **La Mango Restaurant** — Ikeja GRA (Mon–Sat: 9am–5pm)
- **The View Hotel** — Lekki Phase 1 (Mon–Sat: 9am–5pm)

## Membership Benefits (₦45,000/month)

- Unlimited access to all restaurant locations
- Complimentary Wi-Fi
- Periodic breakfast vouchers
- 35% discount on additional meeting room hours
- One complimentary weekend tour
- Meeting rooms for 1 hour monthly at physical offices
- Daily access to multiple coworking locations
- Virtual office package and membership benefits
- Guest policy: three guests daily; first hour free per guest

## FAQ Highlights

Members receive access without being required to purchase from menus. Guests receive complimentary first-hour access before requiring food orders.
