# About Venia Business Hub

Source: https://veniabusinesshub.com/about/

## We Are Engineering The Future Of Work

Venia Business Hub is Nigeria's premier serviced, virtual and coworking space located in Lekki, Lagos. The company provides practical, affordable and flexible solutions to the common difficulties that come with running a business in Nigeria.

## Our Story

Founded in 2011, Venia identified a gap in the market where entrepreneurs struggled to secure accessible, affordable workspace with essential amenities. The founders recognized the need for environments providing power, internet connectivity, and professional ambience.

The company has grown significantly, now housing over 5,000 businesses within its network and supporting hundreds of entrepreneurs. Beyond physical spaces, Venia has cultivated a community where professionals connect and collaborate.

## Our Values

The organization operates around three core principles — **Connect, Create, Collaborate** — supported by five foundational values: **Integrity, Innovation, Community, Resilience, and Hardwork**.
