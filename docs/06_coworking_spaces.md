## Coworking Spaces Created To Boost Your Productivity

Ignite passion using our coworking spaces. Venia takes coworking spaces in Nigeria to a new level. Our shared desks enable collaboration among like-minded entrepreneurs. For private work, we offer breakout pods in lounge areas.

## Service Options

### Coworking
Secure a permanent desk reserved solely for you, with the benefits of your own office in a shared-space setting, access to community events, and business advisory services.

### Hot Desk
Come in whenever and secure a desk for an hour or a couple of days at any of our coworking spaces in Lagos. Pay only for desks needed on a first-come, first-served basis.

### Dedicated Desk
Permanent desk reservation with office benefits in a shared setting, community event access, and business advisory services.
