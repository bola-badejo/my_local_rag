# Venia Business Hub — One-Page Summary

Venia Business Hub is a coworking and office-space provider headquartered in Lekki Phase 1, Lagos, Nigeria. Founded in 2011, Venia has grown to house over 5,000 businesses in its network.

## What they do

Venia provides flexible workspace and business-support services for freelancers, startups, SMEs and multinationals entering the Nigerian market. Core offerings:

- **Serviced offices** (Corporate Elite, Prestige)
- **Coworking** (dedicated desks, hot desks, open spaces)
- **Virtual offices** (Classic, Plus, Premium)
- **Meeting rooms** (meeting room, boardroom, training room)
- **Training venues** (Aqua Hall up to 25 people, Terra Hall up to 100)
- **InstaSpaces** — multi-location workspace membership (₦45,000/month) across Lekki Phase 1 and Ikeja venues
- **Branding & advertising** — paid ads, brand strategy, campaigns, and growth packages (Blaze ₦37k, Sapphire ₦59k, Perk ₦98k monthly)
- **Venia Gateway Services (VGS)** — market-entry support for multinationals

## Guiding principles

Three principles: **Connect, Create, Collaborate.**
Five values: Integrity, Innovation, Community, Resilience, Hardwork.
