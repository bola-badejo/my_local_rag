# Pricing Requests and Booking a Tour

Sources:
- https://veniabusinesshub.com/request-pricing-2/
- https://veniabusinesshub.com/book-a-tour/

## Request Pricing

Public rate cards are not published on the website. Prospective clients submit a short form with name, email, phone number, business industry, and the service of interest (coworking space, virtual package, private office, or meeting room). Venia typically replies in less than 9 hours with a detailed pricing email from a customer representative.

## Book a Tour

Prospective clients can book an in-person tour via the Book-a-Tour form, providing name, email, phone, business industry, preferred date, and service of interest (coworking space, virtual package, or private office).

Venia uses a technology-enabled sign-in / sign-out system for visitors and members.
